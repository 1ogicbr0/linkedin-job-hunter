## Search preferences

- **Roles:** e.g. Full Stack Developer, Frontend, Backend, AI Engineer
- **Core stack to match:** e.g. React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL
- **Not a fit:** e.g. mobile-only, Web3, data-science-only, sales
- **Level:** e.g. mid to senior; skip roles requiring 7+ years
- **Remote:** which remote roles you can legally take (e.g. "worldwide", "EMEA incl. my country")
- **Relocation:** countries you'd move to (with sponsorship), and per-country salary floors
- **Excluded:** countries or company origins to skip
- **Window:** past 24 hours (daily runs) or past week
- **Applicant cap:** 100
- **Search locations (geoId):** see references/search.md
